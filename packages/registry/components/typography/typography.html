The lighthouse keeper's notebook

A short story about patience, weather and the small habits that keep a light burning.

The first winter

Every evening at six the keeper climbed the ninety-two steps, wound the clockwork and wrote the weather in a notebook with a green cover.

"A light that is never late is the only kind a ship can trust."

Things to check each night

  • The lens is clean and the wick is trimmed.
  • The clockwork turns once every 30 seconds.
  • The fog bell answers when struck.

Notes from the keeper's log, 1894.
