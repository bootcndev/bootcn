# Logo marquee

Customer logos that scroll in a loop under a line of social proof, with a pause button.

## Usage

Replace the items with your customers' logos (`<img>` with the company
name as `alt`), in both groups. A static alternative is the Logo cloud
block.

## Accessibility

- A section named by its heading; the strip pauses on hover, focus and its
  button, and stands still under reduced motion.
- The looping copy of the logos is hidden from screen readers.

## Dependencies

- Bootstrap 5.3+ CSS.
- `themes/tokens/tokens.css`.
- The `marquee` component.
